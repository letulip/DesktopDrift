# Step 08 — Poki: submission + the exclusivity decision

**Status:** deliberately deferred · **Wave 4 — decision point, not a task**
**When:** after 4–8 weeks of CrazyGames + Yandex metrics exist. Not before —
the whole point is negotiating (or declining) with numbers in hand.
**Who:** owner decides; promoter prepares the submission + the decision brief.

## Goal

Either a Poki deal that's actually worth its price, or a documented "no" —
never an accidental signature.

## The core tension (verified 07.2026 — sdk.poki.com/deals)

- Poki ≈ 60M MAU, hand-curated; accepted games get real editorial + algorithmic
  push and Poki engineering attention. The biggest single prize on the open web.
- **Default deal = web-exclusive, 5 years:** on the open web the game may then
  exist ONLY on Poki. GitHub Pages, itch, Newgrounds, GameJolt, CrazyGames,
  Yandex — all would have to come down. Steam/mobile stores are exempt.
- Alternative: **non-exclusive one-time flat license fee** — no ongoing rev share.

## How

1. **Collect the decision inputs** (promoter, from CG/Yandex dashboards +
   itch analytics): monthly plays, avg session length, D1/D7 retention where
   available, monthly ad revenue run-rate across all portals.
2. **Submit to Poki** via their submission form (free, non-binding; they answer
   only if interested). The game must pass their bar: responsive 16:9, ≥30 FPS
   on mid mobile, no login walls ✅, kid-safe ✅, no IAP ✅. SDK integration
   happens only AFTER they bite (they assign a team) — do not pre-build a Poki
   adapter.
3. **If Poki responds**, promoter drafts the decision brief:
   - Exclusive: projected Poki revenue must beat (all current portal revenue ×
     5 years) + the option value of every future web channel. Ask Poki for
     comparable-title numbers; they share ranges when serious.
   - Flat fee: compare offer vs ~2 years of current run-rate.
   - Walk-away: keep multi-portal, revisit in a year with better numbers.
4. Owner decides. **The promoter agent never accepts any terms** —
   PROMOTER_AGENT.md hard rule 4 applies doubly here.

## Gotchas

- A Poki *rejection* costs nothing and is even useful signal — submit without fear
  once metrics exist; it's the *acceptance* that forces the real decision.
- If exclusivity is ever signed: itch page, NG, GJ, CG, Yandex takedowns become
  contractual obligations with deadlines — that teardown checklist gets written
  then, not now.

## Done when

Decision brief exists with real numbers and the owner has made an explicit
call (yes-exclusive / yes-flat / no) — recorded in `docs/promo/LOG.md`.
